# FootballGM Sim

A pro football league simulator and GM game that starts from the real league as it stood after the 2025 season, with every name made fictional.

- **GM Mode:** take over one franchise for the 2026 offseason. Hire the coaches whose philosophies decide how your team plays, then build the roster through contracts, the salary cap, free agency, trades, and the draft.
- **Simulation Mode:** hand all 32 teams to AI front offices and fast-forward a week, a season, or a century.

Every game is simulated snap by snap by a play-by-play engine calibrated against real NFL statistics.

> Status: Phase 0 (foundations). Nothing playable yet.
